Download

Simple Social is available on the web, on your phone, and in your terminal. All of them use the same account, so you can sign in anywhere.

Install the app

Add Simple Social to your home screen. It opens full-screen, without the browser's address bar, just like an app from the app store.

To add Simple Social to your Home Screen on iPhone or iPad:

  1. Open this page in Safari (this doesn't work in Chrome or other browsers on iOS).
  2. Tap the Share icon in the toolbar.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top right.

Look in your browser's menu (usually the ⋮ or ☰ icon) for an option called Install App or Add to Home Screen.
