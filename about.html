About Simple Social

Simple Social is a small, no-frills social network. Post text, photos, video, or audio, follow the people you care about, and see their posts in a plain chronological feed.

What you can do

  • Share posts with text and media
  • Follow other people and see their posts in your feed
  • Like and comment on posts
  • Get notified when someone follows you, likes, or comments on your posts

Built to be simple

Every Simple Social app talks to the same open API. The website, the phone apps, and the command-line tools are all separate front ends built on top of it. The API documentation describes everything you need to write your own.
